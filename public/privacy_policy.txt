---

CART (LOCAL STORAGE)

Your shopping cart, your dismissal of the donation notice, and a
short-lived list of items recently found to be unavailable are stored
locally in your browser using localStorage. This data is not accessible
to us and is cleared if you clear your browser data. Only the item IDs,
quantities, and currency in your cart are sent to our server, and only
when you check out or when the Site checks whether items are still
available.

---

BROWSING THE CATALOG

Product listings are fetched from Fourthwall on your behalf through our
server. Browsing the catalog does not send any personal data to Fourthwall
beyond a standard request for product information. Like any website, our
hosting provider (Cloudflare) receives technical information such as your
IP address and browser type when you load the Site. The Site also loads
a font from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), which means your IP
address is sent to Google when the page loads.

---

CHECKOUT

When you click "checkout," our server asks Fourthwall to create a cart
containing only the item IDs and quantities you selected, and you are
then redirected to Fourthwall (https://fourthwall.king-tajin.dev) to
complete your purchase. Any personal or payment information you enter
at checkout - name, address, email, card details, and so on - is
submitted directly to Fourthwall and governed by their own privacy
policy (see "PAGES HOSTED ON FOURTHWALL" below). We receive only the
order information necessary for fulfillment, such as the items ordered
and the order total. We may use this information to send ourselves an
order notification. We do not control or take responsibility for
Fourthwall's data practices.

---

ANALYTICS

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which collects anonymous,
aggregated traffic data. It does not use cookies, does not track you
across sites, and does not collect any personal information. For more
information see https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/

---

COOKIES

store.king-tajin.dev does not use cookies. Pages hosted on Fourthwall
(see below) may use cookies and other tracking technologies, as described
in the Fourthwall-hosted privacy policy.

---

PAGES HOSTED ON FOURTHWALL

Some pages are not part of store.king-tajin.dev and are hosted by
Fourthwall at fourthwall.king-tajin.dev, including checkout, the
donations page, and the returns and FAQ pages. Those pages have their
own privacy policy, which applies to anything you do there:

  https://fourthwall.king-tajin.dev/pages/privacy-policy

That policy describes Fourthwall's use of cookies, analytics, and
advertising technologies, and it takes precedence over this policy for
Fourthwall-hosted pages. This policy applies to store.king-tajin.dev.

---

YOUR RIGHTS

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have the
right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data we
hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email
addresses above.

If you are located in California, you have rights under the California
Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We do not sell your personal data.
